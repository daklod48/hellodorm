# HelloDorm Improvement Lab: rubric (frozen 2026-11-05, run 0)

Product type: app (mobile first web app). 8 criteria, each scored 1 to 10. Total out of 80.
Scored from the captures (phone 390 x 844 and laptop 1440 x 900) and `metrics.json` of each run. Do not change this file after run 0, so scores compare across runs.

| # | Criterion | A 3 looks like | A 6 looks like | A 9 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First impression beauty | Looks like a template or a school exercise; uneven spacing; clashing or flat colours. | Pleasant and consistent; some screens crowded or plain; nothing memorable. | Polished on every screen; a clear visual identity; one would expect it in an app store top list. |
| 2 | Clarity of the main task | Hard to tell what the app is for or what to tap first. | The two main tasks (buy, sell or donate) are findable, but compete with other content. | Within 5 seconds a new visitor knows what HelloDorm does and the next step; one obvious primary action per screen. |
| 3 | Flow without dead ends | Steps lead to errors, blank screens or places with no way forward. | The main flow works; some side paths end without a next step or need guessing. | Every screen offers a clear next step or way back; errors explain themselves; the buy flow reaches a pickup code without confusion. |
| 4 | Trust and credibility | Feels unsafe or fake; unclear who runs it, what happens with money or data. | Basic signals (prices, rules, a demo label) exist but are scattered. | Pickup place, payment safety, who runs it and what is a demo are stated where the decision is made; nothing feels deceptive. |
| 5 | Mobile quality | Phone layout broken, cramped or needs sideways scrolling; key actions far down. | Works at 390 px; some screens long or busy; actions sometimes below the fold. | Feels native on a phone: thumb reachable actions, compact cards, no wasted space, comfortable at 320 to 430 px. |
| 6 | Speed | Slow to show anything; heavy pages (judged from `metrics.json`: ready time, code size). | Ready in about 1 to 2 s on the test machine; moderate code size. | Ready well under 1 s on the test machine; lean code; nothing blocks the first screen. |
| 7 | Accessibility | Low contrast, tiny text or small tap targets visible; check failures in `metrics.json`. | Checks pass but some text is small or dense, or states rely on colour only. | All checks pass (contrast 4.5:1, taps 44 px, text 12 px, field outlines 3:1); clear focus and selected states; readable at a glance. |
| 8 | Delight or innovation | Nothing beyond the basic features. | A few pleasant touches (mascot, small animations) but no distinctive idea. | At least one idea users would talk about; moments that make the dorm community feel real. |
