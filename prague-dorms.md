## Uncertainties

- **Coordinate source:** The coordinates are from Esri ArcGIS, not OpenStreetMap. Every OSM, Nominatim and ČÚZK endpoint was blocked to WebFetch by robots.txt.
- **Official names:** The official pages are mostly in English ("Palachova dormitory", "Strahov Dormitory"). For the Czech names I used the page's own Czech text where it had one (the CUNI address blocks). Otherwise I took them from the Czech URL slugs (for example `masarykova-kolej`, `koleje-strahov`, `dejvicka-kolej`) or the subdomain names (for example `kolejblanice.vse.cz`). The pages themselves don't state those Czech names.
- **Addresses without a house number:** Several CUNI addresses give only one number (Na Větrníku 1932, Zvoníčkova 5, Slavíkova 22, Pátkova 3, Weilova 2). The geocoder resolved them to full addresses: 1932/18, 1927/5, 1499/22, 2136/3 and 1144/2.
- **Kolej 17. listopadu:** The geocoder returned postcode 182 00 instead of the official 180 00, with a score of 98.64. Its point is likely correct but less certain.
- **Strahov:** It is a large complex of many blocks, and Vaníčkova 315/7 is also the address of the ČVUT accommodation office (SÚZ). The point is the address point, not the centre of the complex. The geocoder also returned postcode 169 00, not 160 00.
- **Jarov dorms are close together:** Palachova, Eislerova and Thalerova are within about 250 m of each other, so their stands will crowd together on a map. Vltava and Blanice are about 200 m apart.
- **Two dorms named Vltava:** Charles University also runs a "Kolej Jižní Město – Vltava" (kam.cuni.cz/KAMEN-48.html). The Vltava in the table is the VŠE one, Chemická 953.
- **Not included:** The VŠE University Hotel (Jarov I E), because it is a hotel. Other ČVUT dorms (Bubeneč, Hlávka, Orlík) and other CUNI dorms (Jednota, Budeč, Otava, Komenského, Krystal) exist but I did not verify them.
